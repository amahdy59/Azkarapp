Surah Al-Kahf text, transliteration, and English translation are generated from
[quran-json 3.1.2](https://github.com/risan/quran-json), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The source
attributes its Uthmani text to the Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia, transliteration to
Tanzil.net, and English translation to Saheeh International. Azkarapp adds verse
markers and application metadata.

Concise Arabic explanations for highlighted difficult Quran words come from
[Muyassar of Ghareeb Al-Qur'an](https://qurancomplex.gov.sa/en/techquran/dev/),
published for software developers by the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing
Complex. Azkarapp includes only the entries for the seven complete surahs
currently available in the app (18, 32, 67, 109, 112, 113, and 114), keeps the
reviewed Arabic explanations unchanged, and does not machine-translate them.
The reproducible filter uses the structured conversion in
[Learn-Quran-App](https://github.com/Quran-Summer-of-Code/Learn-Quran-App/tree/1ce3f8194b161e2760eaf6b23235627e1a4ca9fb/Quran),
whose documentation identifies the King Fahd Complex developer release as its
source. The generator verifies that conversion with SHA-1
`561c89c3451ac9e71255a52b493c17db54450da5` before writing the app subset.
