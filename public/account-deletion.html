Delete an wa-zaker account

  1. Open wa-zaker and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Account & data.
  3. Select “Export and delete account.”
  4. Review the confirmation. wa-zaker downloads a JSON export before making the authenticated deletion request.

The account and synchronized application data are permanently deleted. Device-only guest data can be removed separately from Settings.

If you cannot sign in

Deletion from inside the app needs an account you can still reach. If you cannot sign in, email amahdy59@yahoo.com from the address the account uses and ask for it to be deleted. A reply should be expected within 30 days.
