wa-zaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Guest use

wa-zaker can be used without an account. Guest progress and preferences stay in browser storage on the device. Location coordinates used for prayer times remain device-local and are not synchronized to the account service.

Optional accounts

If you choose to sign in, Supabase processes your account identifier and stores the progress, saved azkar, session history, routine completions, and preferences required for backup and cross-device synchronization.

External sign-in providers

Google, Apple, or email sign-in is offered only when configured. Those providers process information under their own policies. wa-zaker does not receive your provider password.

Deletion and export

You can export your local account data and request permanent account deletion from Settings. Deleting an account removes the Supabase Auth user and cascades deletion through application account records.

Contact

Ahmed Mahdy is the data controller for wa-zaker. Questions about this policy, or about the data held for an account, can be sent to amahdy59@yahoo.com.
