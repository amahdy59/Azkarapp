## 3. Fajr/other prayer alarm: choose the intended experience

**Option A — “Set in phone Clock”:** add a user-triggered native bridge using `AlarmClock.ACTION_SET_ALARM` and `com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM`. Pass the calculated time converted to the device clock and a prayer label; handle unavailable Clock apps and leave confirmation visible. This creates a Clock-managed alarm. A fixed daily repeat will not follow changing prayer times; treat it as a specific upcoming alarm and validate next-day/timezone behavior. [Clock intents](https://developer.android.com/guide/components/intents-common#Clock).

**Option B — automatically maintained prayer wake-up alarms:** implement app-owned `AlarmManager` scheduling, exact-alarm access where required, sound/vibration and stop/snooze controls. Recalculate dated prayer instants, cancel obsolete alarms, restore after reboot, react to clock/timezone/settings/access changes, and renew without the WebView running. Background maintenance may replenish the queue; it must not be the precise firing mechanism. Full-screen UI or foreground services need their own platform/Play review if included. Neither option exists yet.
