# Notification hadith shortlist — revised approval draft

**Status. Owner approved the catalogue conditional on authenticity, relevance and appropriate timing.** This replaces the rejected editorial-copy proposal. Source wording and displayed grading checked on 2026-11-05; this is source comparison, not a claim of independent scholarly sign-off.

The owner's requirement is a normal timing/reminder title followed by actual authenticated hadith text. For the three main azkar groups, select a specific zikr already present in that group and show its narrated benefit. Do not invent encouraging sentences to stand in for the hadith.

## Recommended notification anatomy

| Part                       | Prayer                                                                                    | Main azkar group                                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Title                      | `حان وقت صلاة الفجر` or `بقي ١٥ دقيقة على صلاة الفجر`                                     | `حان وقت أذكار الصباح` / `حان وقت أذكار المساء` / `حان وقت أذكار النوم`               |
| Context, only where needed | Identify a Sunnah prayer or the time referred to by the quotation                         | Name the featured zikr, for example `سيد الاستغفار`                                   |
| Body                       | The selected hadith text, with its conditions intact                                      | The hadith about that specific zikr, not a benefit attributed to the whole collection |
| Reference                  | Short collection/number; full narration and context available from its linked destination | Same; keep the specific zikr identifiable when opening the group                      |

Quoted Arabic below is the proposed narration text. Some entries are explicitly **excerpts**, not complete narrations. Punctuation separates the quotation from the caption; an explanatory caption is not attributed to the Prophet ﷺ. The complete cited narration remains available for checking context. Use one selected hadith per alert; alternatives are possible replacements, not extra alerts.

## D. Recommendation for the first approved release

1. Approve P1–P5 and Z1–Z3 as individual selections, or substitute any explicitly identified alternative above. My suggested initial set is the eight main rows; P5b is a particularly good alternative to P5 if the owner prefers the example's mosque encouragement.
2. Keep the normal title and let the hadith provide the reason. Avoid an extra closing instruction such as “open the app now” or “do not miss your reward.”
3. Show a short source beside the quotation and preserve the full narration/context through the destination. A long quotation should expand naturally; do not silently replace it with an editorial paraphrase to fit collapsed notifications.
4. Keep captions which disambiguate the act: Sunnah versus obligatory prayer, Fajr and Asr together, a named zikr rather than the whole group, or reading a whole verse before sleep.
5. No runtime changes, additional preference controls, new alerts, timing changes, rotation or push are approved by this draft. Existing duplicate-delivery investigation remains a separate proposed task.

## Review boundaries and implementation notes

- This draft covers the five prayers and three main groups requested in the owner's correction. Friday, contextual dua, after-prayer and test notifications are not newly approved by omission; their earlier proposal remains unapproved.
- English should be a faithful translation of the selected narration and separately reviewed after Arabic selection; do not revive the rejected generic English encouragement. No English runtime string has changed.
- Existing group membership/counts were checked against `src/app/content/azkar.ts`. New Arabic quotations are source-compared proposals, not edits to the reviewed collection records or the existing prayer-virtue catalogue.
- Some existing repository variants have different wording. For example, the cited Arabic Muslim 657a does not contain `في جماعة`, and Abu Dawud 1269 ends with `حرم على النار`. Do not merge nearby variants into a new purported quotation.
- A full narration or selected complete clause must retain its source identity. The Ayat al-Kursi story needs its correct attribution. Named grades remain visible, including Hasan and Hasan Sahih where applicable.
- Application code, religious content, scheduling, saved state, release notes and publication are unchanged by this review. Documentation-only verification: formatting and source/group mapping inspection; runtime tests are unnecessary for this draft alone.
