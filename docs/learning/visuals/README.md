# Book visuals

The owner requests source-relevant photorealistic graphics and accessible instructional figures across all three books, locally, with rendered phone/desktop evidence. `book-visuals.json` supplies shared authoring metadata. Generated context images are identified as such; they do not document a holy site, depict religious figures, add a ritual pose or prescribe an object/place for worship.

`quiet-prayer-space.webp` and `bedtime-context.webp` were generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-11-27. Lossless source PNGs and complete generation prompts remain under `output/books-audio-review-20261127/graphics/`; the compressed WebP derivatives are project assets. No crop or content alteration is applied. Their subjects are an empty simple prayer space and an empty bedroom at evening. They accompany a chapter transition, outside devotional quotations. All essential learning content remains in selectable text.

Photorealism is used for context, while deterministic diagrams convey source-confirmed directions, counts and sequences. Never put essential labels or Quran/hadith into a bitmap. Graphic placement follows the exact source section; no blanket image appears on every supplication. Native and standalone figures share stable aspect ratios, semantic figure/caption anatomy, bounded responsive width and theme-aware captions. Image failure must preserve the equivalent text and reading controls. New media remains optional and outside installation precache.

Use meaningful alt text for informative images; use an empty alternative for purely decorative imagery. Complex figures retain visible equivalent instructions. Keep labels adjacent to their corresponding features and allow learners to progress at their own pace. These decisions follow [W3C image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/), [complex-image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/) and [Mayer's multimedia-learning principles](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/multimedia-learning/7A62F072A71289E1E262980CB026A3F9/listing).

Existing holy-site photography is documented separately in `../../external-sources/assets/README.md`. Do not relabel inherited or generated imagery as verified documentary photography. Sacred-site geometry, ritual details and physical direction require explicit source comparison before instructional use. Independent scholarly, linguistic and assistive-technology acceptance remain separate from automated checks.
