## Consistent motion and microinteraction plan

The detailed authoritative matrix is at the top of MOTION_SYSTEM.md. The product-level rules are:

1. **Hover shows interactivity.** A quiet border/local highlight and small badge growth acknowledge intent. A fine pointer can hover; touch cannot acquire a sticky hover style. Never scale whole reading text, photos, charts or wide content cards merely because the cursor entered them.
2. **Press acknowledges immediately.** Native action fires without waiting for animation. Existing ordinary/compact press families remain; full-card pseudo-element actions keep their hit/focus geometry still and press only the badge. Keyboard Space/Enter has equivalent feedback.
3. **Focus is unmistakable.** Use the whole-target outline, logical keyboard order and the same optional badge cue. Hover is not a substitute for focus, and color alone is not a selected/complete signal.
4. **Selection remains legible throughout.** Selected text/fill update together. One small finish/panel arrival may accompany the state; no clipped sliding pill, moving text, selection delay or duplicated page capture.
5. **An arrival has one owner.** Route or panel, not route + all cards + nested copy. Ordinary rerenders/search/filter clearing never restart a list of entrances. Loaded return visits do not show a fake new loading step.
6. **Real work owns status.** Busy/progress comes from actual loading/export/download/save state. Success happens on actual success; failure retains entered state and a specific recovery action. No fake progress, count rolling or artificial waiting.
7. **Reversal and cancellation are first-class.** Pointer leave/cancel, Escape, blur, pagehide, hidden state, unmount, disabled state and a newer accepted action cannot leave the old press/cue active. Reuse the existing delegated cleanup and media/page request ownership.
8. **Reading stays stable.** Preserve Mushaf's one finite cancellable paper transition and current page until replacement is ready. Controls remain stationary. No loop, page curl, background parallax, animated blur, extra gesture owner or animation of devotional wording.
9. **Still alternatives are equivalent.** Either app or OS Reduce Motion removes scaling/travel and makes arrivals effectively still. Static focus/highlight/selection/status remains. High contrast, reduced transparency and Forced Colors flatten decorations while keeping protected opaque text layers.
10. **Measure the experience.** Test rapid switching/repeated presses, keyboard/pointer, RTL/LTR, narrow/enlarged and wide layouts in three engines. Preserve frame samples and before/after screenshots. Check layout/hit geometry and actual overlay opacity in addition to axe. Physical touch/assistive technology and field percentiles remain separate acceptance.
