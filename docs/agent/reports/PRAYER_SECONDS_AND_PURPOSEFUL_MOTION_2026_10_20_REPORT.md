# Phase Report — Prayer seconds and purposeful motion

## Objective

Implement the owner's approved reference locally: a single-line mobile seconds countdown without “left”, a small functional progress ring, and a durable positive motion policy with OS/app Reduce Motion alternatives.

## Scope completed

The next Home summary shows localized HH:MM:SS. Its small ring represents elapsed time between the previous and next dated adhan. Changed seconds fade briefly; reduced motion keeps immediate factual updates without the fade or arc interpolation. Home's prayer/domain clock remains minute-based. The owner subsequently approved the independently reproduced WebKit restoration repair. Home now distinguishes focused prayer buttons from the native horizontal-list region and reconciles scroll synchronously when details close. No commit, push, deployment, dependency or persisted-data change.

## Components added or modified

PrayerCountdown is passive presentation. PrayerCard owns one usePrayerCountdown clock shared by its expanded and condensed copies. The model passes only transient previous/next instants. HomeScreen also narrows the interaction-hold predicate and reconciles the existing scroll hook when details close. No new icon library, progress action, timer, focus movement or synthetic scroll write.

## User-visible changes

- Mobile countdowns contain numbers only, on one line. Narrow cards also yield supporting words first; exceptionally narrow enlarged measures yield the decorative icon before digits.
- Prayer names, scheduled times, native browsing and sticky geometry retain their existing typography/behavior.
- The ring advances with actual elapsed prayer-interval time; it never spins or restarts as pretend progress. Seconds use a finite 180ms fade and the ring uses the existing 220ms token.
- Closed prayer cards restore at the top even when WebKit leaves the horizontal browsing region focused. Real keyboard focus on a prayer button still preserves geometry until it leaves, including when it moves to the browsing region. The existing 96px/8px thresholds remain.
- A newly-next card starts at current wall time even if mounted hours earlier. Hidden/offscreen/expired/unmounted cards do no timeout work; returning visibility resynchronizes from the deadline.

## Accessibility work

Arabic digits remain in isolated LTR numeric order. A passive complete localized description names the prayer and remaining time. Decorative visual copies and the ring are hidden from assistive technology; there is no live countdown announcement or extra focusable target. App and OS reduced motion remove the fade/interpolation. Tests cover text fit, stable geometry, keyboard/sticky behavior and axe scans. Actual-device performance and screen-reader acceptance remain manual; automated checks do not establish complete accessibility compliance.

## Tests added or updated

The approved restoration regression simulates native list focus after pointer closure, asserts immediate restoration without another scroll event, verifies subsequent threshold scrolling, and separately retains the keyboard-button hold until blur into the list region. Temporary browser logging established that WebKit leaves `home-all-prayers-row` focused. Logging was removed; the original sticky browser spec has no diff or weakened assertion.

Absolute seconds, fractional rounding, clamping, actual elapsed DST/midnight time, model interval ownership, aligned ticks, suspended-clock recovery, initial-hidden behavior, offscreen cancellation, unmount cleanup, replaced/malformed intervals and inactive-to-next recovery. Component tests verify one timer across both copies and complete Arabic/English descriptions. Browser tests verify fixed-width updates without per-second Home clock changes, mobile words hidden, 320/390/1440px fit, 200% text, condensation, both motion preferences and axe scans.

## Decisions recorded

The owner welcomes beautiful, functional, inexpensive animation by default, with Reduce Motion as a calmer alternative. This supersedes the prayer-summary ban for this small actual-interval ring and the blanket interpretation that recurrent functional updates are prohibited. Decorative ambient loops, flashing, sacred-text distraction and expensive layout/blur effects remain excluded. All work remains local.
