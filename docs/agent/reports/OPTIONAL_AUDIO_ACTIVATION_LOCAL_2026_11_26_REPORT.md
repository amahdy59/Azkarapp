Integrate the three owner-listened OpenDua recordings as an explicit second voice without changing current default reciters, make the existing local review books discoverable, and integrate the subsequently owner-accepted Al-Minshawi/Al-Hussary/Yasser Al-Dosari complete-Al-Mulk recordings. ## Scope completed

- Three approved optional Muhammad Jumah variants retain the exact original bytes, segment mappings, source/CC BY 4.0 evidence and checksum-bound owner statement. No independent scholarly qualification is claimed.
- Published only their three previously absent immutable audio keys. Public SHA-256, MIME, production CORS and 206 byte-range responses verified. Existing objects/defaults/assignments/recordings/timings/asset versions and manifest/cache version 9 remain intact.
- Existing voice selectors/resolver/preferences/offline/export boundaries serve the new choice. Settings explains three-recording coverage; About includes required source credit and licence link. Uncovered readings keep their existing fallback.
- Added six verified waveform arrays only (three dua and three Quran). Preserved previous waveform entries and historical failed-source evidence after the general generator rewrote its failure ledger.
- Confirmed all three Arabic books plus the English Umrah review choice in development. Opened Al-Mukhtar successfully in the user's local tab and left the catalogue visible. Source-conflicted editions remain clearly labelled; production gates are unchanged.
- Prepared complete Al-Mulk 67:1–30 originals for exactly the three selected Quran reciters, with provider-copying permission evidence, exact media and recording-specific provider timing hashes. Generated and verified a concrete local comparison alongside current Abdullah Muhammad. The owner then replied “I approve all”; appended exactly those three Al-Mulk variants, retained Abdullah Muhammad as default, and verified their immutable hosted originals. Other Quran ranges and all provider word timings remain unchanged.
