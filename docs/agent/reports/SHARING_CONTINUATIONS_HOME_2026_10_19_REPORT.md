# Phase Report — Complete sharing and Home attribution

## Objective

Apply the owner's sharing refinements locally: complete Benefits images, sentence-aware continuation, Mushaf lettering, common size options with platform tags, localized numbering, a Home share action matching Reload, and missing prophetic attribution. No commit, push, R2 upload or deployment.

## Scope completed

- Long readings become measured numbered cards; fitting sentences stay together. Collection pagination first moves a following sentence to a fresh card, then considers an existing clause/pause if a sentence alone exceeds the selected format. No reviewed punctuation or wording is inserted, shortened or removed.
- Every intermediate card identifies earlier and later text. First/final cards show the applicable continuation cue. Sources and headings repeat when sources are selected; Text/ZIP keep the complete original payload without duplicate narration identities.
- Share-all includes all 21 hadith Benefits independently of the 15-card screen batch. Arabic/English payloads switch together, English quotations use LTR, and evidence no longer inherits an azkar repetition badge.
- Story 1080×1920, Square 1080×1080, Portrait 1080×1350 and Tall 1080×2920 use one shared selector in both image studios, with two localized destination tags each. Ayah sharing gains Story/Tall. Short individual Benefits use centered Square cards and compact previews follow their aspect ratio.
- Benefits/Home/Wird Quran sharing resolves existing QCF excerpt identities and the approved per-page Mushaf font. Exact glyph/source offsets preserve semantic text through continuations; QCF ink receives extra inline clearance.
- Home Share uses Reload's borderless circular 44px target and 16px icon, emphasized with accent ink. It lazily opens the studio and retains the displayed source. Missing prophetic attribution appears separately from the reviewed quotation, excluding Quran, Companion reports and reported actions. Pointer triggers explicitly retain focus for Safari close recovery.
- English card/part/repetition labels use Western digits; Arabic uses Arabic-Indic digits. Original devotional strings, URLs, QR payloads and filenames remain unchanged.
