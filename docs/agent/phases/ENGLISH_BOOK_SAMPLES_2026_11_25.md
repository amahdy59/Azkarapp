# English book samples with paired Qur'an — 2026-11-25, local

## Owner decision

The owner agrees to the proposed sample-first English translation workflow and requires Qur'anic passages to remain in **both Arabic and English in the English edition**. This applies to embedded passages inside hadith/guidance, excerpts and standalone quotations, in HTML and PDF. English is explicitly labelled translation of meaning and attributed to its translator. Never substitute an English paraphrase for the Arabic original or silently expand an excerpt into a complete verse/surah.

## Controlled plan

1. Prepare one representative English sample per book from the existing Arabic publication, preserving exact selected Arabic Qur'an and source identifiers. Use the existing Saheeh International corpus for corresponding English meanings; identify contiguous excerpts explicitly. Translate the surrounding selected prose/hadith as a draft and keep source comparison records and primary-source links.
2. Generate three self-contained reflowing HTML files and three tagged PDFs from one authoring source. Preserve shared visual language/icons, native semantics, independent Arabic/English language/direction, text enlargement and print pagination. Keep draft/review status visible; no live-app catalogue or reviewed Arabic changes.
3. Verify every selected Arabic/English pair against its source; test generation invariants, responsive/keyboard/axe behavior, render saved PDFs and inspect every page. Record results and deliver samples for translation/style review.

## Boundaries and acceptance

This is the approved sample checkpoint, not a claim that the complete books have been translated or independently reviewed. Unselected/continuing glossary material is excluded explicitly. No publication, dependency, app persistence, synchronization or main navigation change. Preserve independent Home/Settings/document work. Full-book translation and app language-selection integration follow sample/style approval and the existing content-review process.
